THE DARK PAST

An acknowledgment for a project is a formal section to express gratitude to those who provided guidance, support, and help in completing the project. You should begin with a general thank you, then mention specific individuals like teachers, mentors, classmates, and family, clearly stating their contributions. Keep the language formal but sincere, maintain a logical flow, and proofread for errors before concluding the section with a final note of appreciation. An acknowledgement for a book is a section, usually at the front or back, where an author thanks individuals, organizations, or groups who provided emotional, financial, or academic support and contributed to the book's creation or publication process. To write one, you should list everyone who helped, write a specific and sincere paragraph for key contributors, and add personality to make it your own while asking for permission before name.
